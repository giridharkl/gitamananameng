{\mfontCalibri\small Life presents each of us with unique challenges and trials. On the battlefield of \emph{Kurukshetra}, when Arjuna faced one of the greatest moral and existential crises, Lord Krishna imparted to his anguished disciple the sacred teaching of discovering the spiritual within the mundane — delivered in ways both succinct and practical. These sublime teachings, the very essence of the Upanishads, were passed down to us by Sage Vyasa in the form of the \emph{Srimad Bhagavad Gita}, the Divine Song.\\

Your circumstances may differ from those of Arjuna; your challenges may be varied, and your path may be uncertain. Yet the universal teachings of the \emph{Gita} offer every seeker of truth a model for self-improvement and spiritual growth. The \emph{Srimad Bhagavad Gita} is not meant solely for dedicated spiritual aspirants — it also serves as a practical handbook for everyday life. Anyone seeking to balance work and family responsibilities, maintain mental peace, and attain freedom from stress will find its teachings invaluable.\\

This workbook-style journal is not intended to impart the teachings of the \emph{Gita} or to offer commentary — many revered \emph{gurus} and scholars have already done so with great depth. Instead, \emph{Gita Mananam} is designed to help you contemplate the \emph{Gita’s} teachings and integrate them into your daily life.
\newpage
Rooted in the Upanishadic spirit of the \emph{Gita}, the journal honours the traditional practice of \emph{Mananam}—the reflective assimilation of what has already been heard (\emph{śravaṇam}) or studied (\emph{adhyayanam}).\\

This journal invites you to explore the wisdom of the \emph{Gita}, deepen your understanding, and apply its timeless insights to your personal and spiritual journey. The reflective questions accompanying selected \emph{ślokas} are crafted to help you uncover personal meaning within the teachings, fostering greater clarity amid life’s circumstances.\\

Just as Lord Krishna inspired Arjuna to engage in his righteous battle, may the Lord inspire you through this work to navigate your own daily duties with courage and discernment. The eternal purpose of the \emph{Srimad Bhagavad Gita}, the celestial song, is to help you find inner peace and personal growth while remaining anchored in the Divine—even as you fulfil your worldly responsibilities.\\
