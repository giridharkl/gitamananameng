{\mfontCalibri\small Each of us must face our battles in life. On the battlefield of Kurukshetra, Lord Krishna gave to his anguished disciple Arjuna the most sacred teachings of finding the spiritual in the mundane, in ways both succinct and practical. These sublime teachings, the essence of the Upanishads, have been handed down to us by Sage Vyasa in the format of the Bhagavad Gita, the ‘Divine Song.’ \\

Our circumstances may be different from that of Arjuna, and so also our challenges, but the universal teachings of the Gita present every seeker of truth with a model for self-improvement and spiritual progress. 
The teachings of the Gita are not just a spiritual teaching meant for dedicated spiritual seekers, but also a handbook for life. Anyone seeking to balance work and family responsibilities with mental peace and freedom from stress will find these teachings invaluable.\\

This diary and journal based-book is not an attempt to impart the teachings of Gita, nor to provide any commentaries, as several Gurus and scholars have already done that. This Gita Mananam Journal is an attempt to allow you to contemplate the teachings of the Gita, and to make them your own. The Sanskrit word “mananam” is used in this context as a process of contemplation on what has already been heard (shravanam) or read.\\

So, this journal is designed to give you the space to express yourself and to apply the teachings in your life. The questions on the Gita shlokas are framed to allow you to find personal meaning within the context of these teachings and thereby find greater clarity within the circumstances of your life.\\

We hope that as Lord Krishna inspired Arjuna to fight his righteous battle, so may the Lord inspire you through this work, in your own daily duties of life. To accomplish your duties successfully without neglecting your inner peace, to progress personally, and to remain anchored in the Divine, is the eternal purpose of this Celestial Song.\\}
